1. About Data Deletion

Auto Post Manager helps an authorized user publish and manage video content on Facebook Pages through the Meta Graph API. Depending on usage, the application may process publishing records, video files, post descriptions, Page identifiers, and related technical information.

2. How to Request Deletion

To request deletion of information associated with Auto Post Manager, send an email to the application operator:

Email: nafisagung67@gmail.com

Use the subject line: Data Deletion Request - Auto Post Manager

Please describe the information you want deleted and provide enough detail for the operator to identify the relevant records. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or other authentication secrets in your request.

3. Processing Requests

The operator will review the request and, where appropriate, delete the relevant information under the operator's control. Additional information may be requested to verify the request and prevent unauthorized deletion.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and operational purposes.

4. Local Application Data

Some application data may be stored locally on the operator's computer or server. If you operate your own installation, you may also need to delete locally stored records and files under your control.

5. Data Held by Meta

This process covers information under the application operator's control. Information retained by Meta is subject to Meta's own policies and data deletion procedures. Requests concerning data held by Meta may need to be submitted directly to Meta.
