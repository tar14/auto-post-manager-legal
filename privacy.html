Privacy Policy

Application: Auto Post Manager

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Auto Post Manager is a tool designed to help its authorized user manage and publish video content to Facebook Pages through the Meta Graph API.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the application may process and how that information is handled.

2. Information We Process

Depending on how the application is used, it may process Facebook Page identifiers, video files, post descriptions, publishing status information, and identifiers returned by the Meta Graph API. It may also use access tokens required to perform authorized publishing operations.

The application processes only information necessary for its intended publishing and management functions.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Publish video content to an authorized Facebook Page.
  • Submit post descriptions and other publishing information.
  • Record publishing results and troubleshoot technical errors.
  • Maintain the application's publishing workflow.

4. Storage and Security

Application data may be stored locally on the operator's computer or server. Reasonable measures should be taken to protect access tokens, video files, and other application data against unauthorized access.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Sharing of Information

Information required for publishing is transmitted to Meta platforms through the Meta Graph API. The application does not sell personal information to third parties.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to support publishing history, troubleshooting, and application operation. The operator may delete locally stored information when it is no longer needed.

To request deletion of information associated with the application, contact the application operator using the contact details below.

7. Third-Party Services

The application integrates with Meta services. Use of Facebook and related services is also subject to Meta's own terms and privacy policies.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
